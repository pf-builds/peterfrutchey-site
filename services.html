What I can help with

Everything below is a fixed-price deliverable, scoped in writing before it starts, so you know the cost up front and can stop after any step. Fees depend on scope, and I'm glad to give you definitive numbers once we know the direction. Start with a free call.

AI Enablement
Where AI actually fits in your work, and how to use it safely. Vendor-neutral, no platform to sell.
AI opportunity review. I find the two or three places AI would actually save your team time, ranked by effort and payoff. You get a clear plan you can act on, with or without me.
AI agent or automation pilot. I design and build a working AI assistant for one specific workflow, scoped so you can see it earn its keep before going further.
AI adoption program. A time-boxed rollout: training, usage measurement, and iteration so the tool actually gets used.
AI risk and governance readiness. Guardrails so nothing unsafe ships on its own: where humans stay in the loop, what gets recorded, and a prioritized fix list.
AI capabilities workshop. A half or full day teaching your team to use AI in their actual work, with customized exercises built from your workflows. You leave with a prioritized list of where AI fits and how to start.
Advisory & Strategy
Assess how a business or function actually runs, find the opportunity or the break, and recommend the path. Operations, service and support, knowledge, and the people running it.
Operations & process review. I map how work really flows, find the waste and rework, and recommend a cleaner way to run it. Work like this has cut transfers 20% and contacts 9% on a team.
Support & self-service assessment. I find where a help center or self-service could cut the questions reaching your team, and what it would take. Guided to a 7% drop in contacts, ~90% of visitors never needing to follow up.
Content & knowledge strategy. I evaluate how your content is created, maintained, and used across the organization, then lay out what a clean, governed content system looks like. Built a 200+ article public help center and a 4,000-article internal knowledge base.
Operating model, metrics, and support-function design. How a service or operations function should be structured, what to measure, and a launch plan your team can run.
Escalations and voice-of-customer design. Intake and triage for hard cases, and a feedback program that routes what customers say to the people who can fix it.
Leadership and hiring process design. Supervisor onboarding, manager development, and structured interviewing so the people side keeps up with the process side.
Go-to-market advisory. Package and price your own expertise into offerings your market will buy, such as taking a proven program to other organizations.
Advisory retainer. An ongoing outside set of expert eyes for a founder or operations lead. A standing call each month and someone to think the hard calls through with, within a set block of hours.
Research & Analysis
For the decision in front of you: does the idea hold up, what's already out there, and what should you do about it.
Feasibility & options study. Does the idea work, for whom, and at what cost? Market and demand, competitors, the compliance picture, build versus buy, and options with a straight recommendation.
Market & competitor analysis. Who else is in the space, what they charge, and where the gaps are.
Build-versus-buy analysis. License, configure, or build your own, laid out with honest tradeoffs and a recommendation.
Idea stress-test & business case. I pressure-test a concept and hand you a defensible business case with the risks named, not hidden.
Vendor selection advisory. I evaluate candidate vendors and platforms against what you actually need and recommend the fit.
Implementation & Delivery Advisory
Help making it real without becoming your dev shop. I sit on your side of the table through the build.
Requirements translation. I turn your vision into a plan and specification a vendor or development team can build from, so nothing gets lost between you and them.
Vendor and dev-team representation. Selection criteria, service levels, quality checks, and honest translation both ways, so you're never sold the wrong thing.
Rollout & readiness review. Before you onboard a big customer or launch new software, I pressure-test whether you're actually ready and flag what will break. Delivered readiness across dozens of enterprise implementation projects.
Testing and rollout validation. Test strategy, workflow validation, and defect tracking so the launch lands the way it was supposed to.
Change management for new tools. The people side of a rollout, so the thing you paid for gets used.
Knowledge base and procedure build-out. Stand up, move, or reorganize a knowledge base, and turn tribal knowledge into written procedures your team and your AI tools can rely on.
Workshops
A day in the room, with prep beforehand and a written action plan afterward.
Process workshop facilitation. I prep the workflow analysis, build the agenda, and run the on-site session with your cross-functional leaders. We drive to decisions, and you get a written action plan out of it. Facilitated process-improvement workshops for 20+ senior leaders.
AI capabilities workshop. Half or full day, built from your real workflows. Listed under AI Enablement above.
